## Context

The product plan included email-gated downloads.

## Decision

Downloads are ungated by default. The gating option was never built, so gating is not available rather than merely unused.

## Why

It matches the Hub's "generous by default" stance: a free library that helps whether or not the reader ever hires the agency.
